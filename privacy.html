Privacy & visitor statistics

This portfolio uses a private analytics database to understand how its work is viewed. It records the network IP address reported by the hosting gateway, visit time, pages viewed, referring website hostname, browser information, and time for which each page is visible. It does not identify your name or track activity on other websites.

Random session identifiers distinguish visits, with a new visit after 30 minutes of inactivity or in a new tab. They are stored in your browser's session storage. An IP address may be shared or changed and is not proof of a person's identity. Time measurements are approximate and may be interrupted by connection loss, browser settings or blocking software.

Records are stored in Supabase in Canada, accessible only to the portfolio owner, and removed after 90 days by a daily cleanup. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected. No advertising cookies are used.
